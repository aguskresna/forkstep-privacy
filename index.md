# Forkstep — Privacy policy

*Last updated: 22 November 2026.*

Forkstep is an app that shows what is in food, what activities burn, and
body measures such as BMI. This policy says what happens to your
information. In short: **Forkstep collects nothing. Everything stays on your
phone.**

## What Forkstep keeps, and where

- **Your details** that you type in (age, sex, height, weight, waist, resting
  heart rate, activity level, pregnancy or breastfeeding, wheelchair use,
  goal weight) and your settings are stored **only on your phone**, in the
  app's own storage. They are used only to work out the numbers you see.
- **The caffeine log** is kept on your phone for 48 hours, then deleted.
- **Family and prices**, if you add them: the names, ages, sex and
  pregnancy of the people you cook for (You > Family), the prices you
  type for foods, and a walking loop length. Stored only on your phone,
  used only for the numbers you see, removed with the app.
- **Pictures you share**: the share button makes a picture of a result
  and opens your phone's own share menu. Nothing is sent unless you pick
  an app and send it yourself.
- **The meal check** is kept only while the app is open. It is never saved.
- Nothing is stored anywhere else. Forkstep has **no account, no server, no
  analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting**. It does not send your
  information to us or to anyone.

## Health Connect (Android)

If you choose to connect Health Connect, Forkstep **reads** three things on
your phone, and only after you allow each one:

- **steps today**, for the Steps tool;
- **your latest weight**, only when you tap "Use my latest weight", which
  then becomes the weight in your details;
- **active calories today**, shown next to the Calories burned estimate.

Forkstep never writes to Health Connect, never sends this data off your
phone, and never shares, sells or uses it for advertising. You can remove
access at any time in the Health Connect app; Forkstep keeps working without
it.

## Notifications

If you turn on water reminders, Forkstep asks to show notifications. The
reminders are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere.

## Starting language

On the first launch on Android, Forkstep reads the country of your SIM card
or mobile network (no permission needed) only to choose the starting
language (when your phone's language is not one the app has) and the daily
targets of your country (Malaysia or Indonesia). The country is not stored
or sent anywhere; you can change both in the app.

## Internet

Forkstep works offline. It does not need the internet and does not contact
any server. (The food data is built into the app.)

## Children

Forkstep is not made for children. It has no sign-up and collects nothing,
from anyone. Some tools hide adult results for people under 18.

## Deleting your information

In the app, go to **You > Your details > Clear my details**. Uninstalling the
app deletes everything it stored.

## Not medical advice

Forkstep is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or
prevent any medical condition. Consult a healthcare professional before
changing your diet or exercise.
